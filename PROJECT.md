# Fragments

## Concept

This site is a quiet personal writing space. It should not introduce the owner directly like a profile or portfolio. It should suggest identity through atmosphere, quotation, fragments, and aesthetic choices.

The homepage is an entrance, not an explanation.

## Current Direction

- Minimal personal site.
- Main section is called `Fragments`, not `Blog`.
- No direct self-introduction on the homepage.
- No research keyword list on the homepage.
- No visible portfolio or academic profile until the concept requires it.
- A slight gothic tone is allowed, but it should stay restrained.

## Visual Mood

- Dark background.
- Off-white text.
- Small amount of deep red.
- Large silence and empty space.
- Text should feel placed, not explained.

## Current Structure

- `index.html`: landing page with a small mark and the `Fragments` entrance.
- `blog/index.html`: list page for fragments.
- `_posts/`: writing entries managed by Jekyll.
- `_config.yml`: GitHub Pages / Jekyll configuration.

## Writing Rules

- Prefer short fragments over direct explanation.
- Do not over-describe the author.
- Avoid turning the site into a resume.
- Avoid putting non-original quotes in central positions unless their source is clear.
- When a deadline or school project is involved, prioritize completion and clarity over atmosphere.
