# Editing Fragments

Fragments live in `_posts`.

To create a new one, run:

```powershell
node scripts/new-fragment.mjs "Title Here"
```

Then open the new file in `_posts` and replace the body text.

Front matter fields:

- `title`: the visible title.
- `symbol`: a small hover label on the Fragments page, such as `dream`, `wine`, `room`, `street`, or `ashes`.
- `accent`: the article accent color.

Keep entries short unless the fragment really wants more space.
